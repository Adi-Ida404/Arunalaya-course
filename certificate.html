About the Program

The Certified Sports Physiotherapy & Sports Rehab Specialist (CSPRS) program is designed for physiotherapists aiming to enhance their expertise in sports injury management, rehabilitation, and performance enhancement. Led by Dr. Chakshu Bansal, a global leader in sports therapy and rehabilitation, this course provides a comprehensive, clinical approach to treating and preventing sports injuries.This program covers advanced assessment techniques, manual therapies, exercise prescription, taping methods, and rehabilitation protocols used by top sports therapists worldwide.

Whether you are managing athletic injuries, chronic pain, or rehabilitation, this course equips you with 200+ Physiotherapy techniques and real-world clinical applications to elevate your practice.
