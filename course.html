About the Program

The Certified Tapedia Expert (CTE) course is a comprehensive deep dive into the art and science of taping techniques. Designed for physiotherapists, sports therapists, and healthcare professionals, this course explores Kinesiology, Rigid, Semi-elastic, and Sports taping applications.

Learn to assess, diagnose, and apply taping techniques for musculoskeletal, neurological, paediatric, women health, bio-mechanical and postural dysfunctions. Whether you are managing athletic injuries, chronic pain, or rehabilitation, this course equips you with 200+ taping techniques and real-world clinical applications to elevate your practice.

  • Introduction to Tapedia and Taping Concepts
  • Understanding Different Types of Tapes
  • Physiology Behind Taping
  • Principles of Taping Techniques
  • Application of Taping for Muscles, Tendons, Ligaments & Joints
  • Advanced Taping for Clinical Conditions
  • Taping for Postural, Neurological Dysfunctions, Paediatric and Women Health
