About the Program

The Certified Tapedia Expert (CTE) course is a comprehensive deep dive into the art and science of taping techniques. Designed for physiotherapists, sports therapists, and healthcare professionals, this course explores Kinesiology, Rigid, Semi-elastic, and Sports taping applications. Learn to assess, diagnose, and apply taping techniques for musculoskeletal, neurological, paediatric, women health, bio-mechanical and postural dysfunctions. Whether you are managing athletic injuries, chronic pain, or rehabilitation, this course equips you with 200+ taping techniques and real-world clinical applications to elevate your practice.

What You Will Learn

  • Learn Kinesiology, Rigid, Semi-elastic Taping.
  • Understand the Physiology for effective treatment.
  • Practical demonstrations with clinical case studies.
  • Application in 200+ conditions.
  • Perform biomechanical taping.
  • Advanced techniques like combination taping.
